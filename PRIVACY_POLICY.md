# Privacy Policy

**Tiny Shelf**  
**Last updated: October 30, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Tiny Shelf (the “App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile game, including through integrated advertising and analytics services.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

The App collects and shares the following information with its integrated service providers and advertising partners:

- **Device IDs / Advertising ID:** Device identifiers, including the Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android when available, are collected and shared to support app functionality, analytics, advertising delivery, and advertising measurement.
- **Approximate Location / IP Address:** IP addresses are collected and shared and may be used to determine your approximate location, such as your country, region, or city. This refers to location inferred from an IP address rather than precise GPS coordinates.
- **Usage and technical information:** Integrated SDKs may collect app interactions, session information, ad impressions and clicks, app version, operating system, and device information to support operation, analytics, and advertising.

We and our integrated partners use this information for the following purposes:

- **App Functionality:** Operate and maintain the App and its integrated services, support reliable performance, and detect or prevent fraud and abuse.
- **Analytics:** Understand how the App is used, measure engagement and advertising performance, and improve the user experience.
- **Advertising & Marketing:** Deliver relevant advertisements, personalize advertising where permitted and subject to applicable consent requirements, limit ad frequency, and measure campaign effectiveness.

The availability of particular identifiers and the extent of processing depend on your device settings, privacy choices, region, and SDK configuration.

### Game Progress and Analytics

Game progress, including completed levels, unlocked areas, and in-game preferences, is stored locally on your device. You do not need to create an account to save progress this way. We do not upload your local game save to servers operated by us.

The App also uses Firebase Analytics to understand usage. Configured analytics events may include sessions, in-game interactions, level completion, and area unlocks. These events are separate from your locally stored game save and do not constitute game-save synchronization.

## 2. Third-Party Services & Ad Networks

The App integrates **Google AdMob** and third-party advertising networks through **mediation partners**, including **Pangle, Unity Ads, InMobi, and ironSource**. These services facilitate ad selection, delivery, and measurement within the App.

Through their integrated SDKs, these partners collect and receive information described above, including device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and approximate location derived from IP addresses, subject to applicable settings and privacy choices. Their processing is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Please review the providers’ privacy policies for details about their data practices and available privacy controls:

- **Google AdMob:** [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Pangle:** [Pangle Privacy Policy](https://www.pangleglobal.com/privacy)
- **Unity Ads:** [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- **InMobi:** [InMobi Privacy Policy](https://www.inmobi.com/privacy-policy)
- **ironSource:** [ironSource Privacy Policy](https://www.is.com/privacy-policy/)

For analytics, see the [Firebase Analytics Policy](https://firebase.google.com/policies/analytics) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. Advertising and Your Privacy Choices

Advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized, depending on your region, applicable consent requirements, privacy choices, and advertising configuration. Non-personalized advertising may still involve processing information such as IP addresses and device information for ad delivery, security, and measurement.

You can manage available advertising ID and ad personalization controls in your device’s privacy settings. Available options and their names vary by operating system and version. Where the App provides privacy or consent controls, you can use those controls to manage your choices, including withdrawing consent where applicable. Changing advertising preferences does not necessarily remove advertisements from the App.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent. To make a request concerning our processing, contact us using the details below. For information processed by advertising or analytics providers, their policies explain the relevant request procedures.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game progress remains on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App. Doing so may permanently remove progress that has not been backed up. Copies retained by your operating system or backup provider are subject to that provider’s settings and policies.

Advertising and analytics providers retain information according to their respective policies, processing purposes, and applicable legal requirements. Deleting the App or its local data does not automatically delete information already collected by these providers. Consult their privacy policies for retention information and deletion options, or contact us for assistance with a privacy request.

## 5. Data Security

**All data transmitted through the App’s advertising SDKs, including Google AdMob and its integrated mediation partners, is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.** This encryption helps protect information against unauthorized interception during transmission.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information processed. The protection of locally stored game progress also depends on your device and any backup services you use. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

Tiny Shelf is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. Advertising and analytics SDKs process information as described in this policy.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action, including working with relevant service providers where necessary.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, integrated services, or data practices. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Where required by applicable law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for relevant changes.
